What the Data Shows

The analysis shows that public toilet accessibility is not only about the number of toilets available. A municipality may have many toilets, but that does not necessarily mean better access for disabled users or better availability throughout the day.

The visualizations combine accessibility rates, population context, spatial distribution, opening hours, and facility-based search to give a more complete picture of public toilet access.

Accessibility varies

Some municipalities have a much higher share of disabled-accessible toilets than others.

Counts can mislead

København has many toilets, but raw counts do not fully explain accessibility quality.

Time matters

A toilet is only useful when it is open, so opening hours are part of accessibility.

User needs differ

Accessibility depends on features such as wheelchair access, changing tables, water, and free access.
